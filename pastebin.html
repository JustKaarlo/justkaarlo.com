          Which pastes can I open?
          
Use a Pastebin.ai link, such as https://pastebin.ai/p/PASTE_ID, or just its ID. Available
            public and unlisted pastes can be read here. Private, password-protected, encrypted, and burn-after-read
            pastes must be opened on Pastebin.ai.

          
Copy link creates a link to this viewer on your domain. You can also add
            ?id=PASTE_ID to the page address. Viewing preferences stay in this browser; paste text is not
            saved here.
